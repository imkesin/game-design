### Outbid Action Selection

- **Status:** seed
- **One-liner:** Claim action spaces by playing numbered cards from hand; anyone (including you) can
  add occupants by playing a strictly higher card.
- **Inspired by:** ?

#### Idea

Each player holds a hand of numbered cards (e.g. 1–7). On your turn you select an action by playing
a card onto that zone. If a zone is already occupied, you may also visit it — but only by playing a
card of higher value than the one sitting there. Playing your maximum (the 7) locks the space
against everyone, yourself included.

Twist: some zones want _low_ numbers instead — the lowest card wins or the space only accepts cards
under a threshold. Now your 1 and 2 aren't dead weight; the same hand pulls in two directions.

#### Why it's Interesting

- Your hand is a budget: the highest value is total control of one space, but you only have one, and
  spending it early telegraphs weakness everywhere else.
- If you want to visit a place multiple times, then you need to lead with lower values.

#### Decisions

- Action should resolve immediately; better flow of the game
- Cards placed probably need to stay near the zones; there's a possibility of "bumping," but not the
  direction I'm going for here.

#### Points of Interaction

- If cards or workers stay out, then you can interact with the number of people who have take the
  action already.

#### Questions

- Hand refresh cadence: full reset each round, or cards spent stay spent across rounds?
- With 1–7 and N players, how many zones before the math gets solvable/degenerate?
