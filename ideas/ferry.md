# Ferry (working title)

- **Status:** seed
- **One-liner:** Each player is a ferryperson for the dead, running a pick-up-and-deliver loop along
  a shared one-way river to guide souls to the afterlife.
- **Inspired by:** Maracaibo (one-directional loop movement), rondels.

## Kernel

Pick-up-and-deliver. Souls appear at pick-up points; you carry them to the afterlife.

The river is **shared and one-way**: a rondel-like loop/track everyone moves along in the same
direction. Stops are limited, so ferries **block** each other when contending for the same location.

Everything else is undesigned.

## Open Questions

- What differentiates souls (destination, weight, urgency, fare)? Undecided.
- Blocking: is a stop exclusive (one ferry) or capacity-limited? Can you pass an occupied stop, or
  must you stop behind it? Can you pay to skip or force ahead?
- Movement: fixed step, variable (Maracaibo-style spend to move further), or card-driven?
- Loop vs. line: does the river cycle (pick up again after delivering), or is the afterlife a
  terminus you return from?
- What constrains the ferry (capacity, time, fuel)?
- What is scored?
