**Pitch — Fated: The Art of the Love Story**

1. In Fated, players are rival authors aiming to write the next great modern romantasy.
2. Authors explore possibilities, develop traits of their protagonists, and contend for unique
   scenes to shape their love story.
3. Players race to fill out six defining scenes, but the winner is whoever earns the most Love from
   the quality and depth of their characters' romance.
