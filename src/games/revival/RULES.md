# Revival

## Overview

In Revival, you manage a construction company cashing in on a city in its second act. A new mayor
has been elected, determined to turn the city around, and is willing to engage the private sector.

The city is run down: every empty plot is a blighted lot. Each turn, your company must choose one:
break ground on a new development, or finish an existing project. The mayor will walk the streets to
inspect your work, leaving tax incentives on empty lots and taxing the construction sites he passes.

The company with the most money at the end of the mayor's term will be declared the winner.

## Setup

1. **City**: lay out the board.
2. **Tiles**: sort the 96 building tiles by level into 3 bags: **L1, L2, L3**. Parks go in a supply.
3. **Historical**: shuffle the 8 setup cards and deal one beside each quadrant, upright. Place every
   building on it whose badge is at least the number of players (no badge: always): an L1 tile of
   its type from the L1 bag, finished side up. A historical Park is a supply Park, finished side up.
4. **Mayor**: stand the mayor on City Hall.
5. **Agenda**: shuffle the 24 agenda cards and count out the game's deck (table); put the rest away
   unseen.
6. **Row**: deal the row (below). Each slot adds to its tile's cost: **+0, +0, +1, +1, +2, +2, +3,
   +3**.

| Players | Tiles in row | Agenda cards | Historical buildings |
| ------- | ------------ | ------------ | -------------------- |
| **2**   | 5            | 13           | 20                   |
| **3**   | 6            | 13           | 16                   |
| **4**   | 7            | 12           | ~14                  |
| **5**   | 8            | 11           | 12                   |

7. **Companies**: each player takes a company mat (credit rating 0) and 3 workers. The first player
   gets 4 coins, the second 5, and so on.
8. **Opening walk**: reveal the top agenda card. The first player walks the mayor there from City
   Hall: **Target** and **Stops** only (below). Discard it, then reveal 2 face up beside the board:
   the mayor's agenda.

## The row

**Deal the row** into its first slots (table), construction side up, from the +0 end, drawing blind:
every slot but the last from the **lowest bag** that still has tiles; the **last slot** from the
**next bag up** (L3 when L3 is the lowest). A bag that runs out partway: carry on from the next.

## The city

The city is a grid of **blocks** of 1–4 plots, with streets between them. The **corners** of an
intersection are the 4 plots touching it, one in each block round it; every plot is a corner of
exactly one intersection. City Hall (C3) has no corners: its plots are the plaza.

## Your turn

Do **one** action. You can't pass.

- **Break ground** on an empty plot: put down a tile and as many of your workers as it needs: **Park
  1, Residential 1, Commercial 2, Industrial 3**.
  - **Row tile**: pay its cost (gold coin) plus its slot's surcharge.
  - **Park**: free, from the supply. Discard the tile in the +0 slot.
  - Slide the row's tiles toward +0 to close the gap.
  - Take any incentives on the plot.
- **Work** a line of your projects: start at any of them and go one way along its row or column
  (across streets too), taking **1 worker off each** project in turn. Stop whenever you like; the
  line ends at the first plot that isn't your project (empty, a rival's, finished, the plaza).
  - Taking a project's **last** worker **finishes** it: flip the tile and **sell it** (below) right
    away, before moving on. If it's one of the mayor's corners, take 1 incentive.

**End of turn**: if **2 or fewer tiles** are left in the row, you're the **walker**: the mayor
walks. Then discard every tile left in the row and deal a fresh one.

## Selling

A building sells for its **base** (on its tile) plus its **count**, in coins. Only **finished**
buildings count; a tile with a worker on it counts for nothing. Historical buildings count.

Count what the building draws on in **its own block twice**. From L2, also count them **once** in
**neighboring blocks** (across a street, not diagonal), your pick: **L1** none, **L2** one, **L3**
two. Each one counted pays:

- **Park**: sells for 1; counts nothing.
- **Residential**: **+1** per **Park**. **Can't be finished until every plot in its block has a
  tile** (with workers or not).
- **Commercial**: **+2** per **Residential**.
- **Industrial**: **+4** per **Commercial**.

## The mayor's walk

1. **Target**: the walker picks one of the 2 face-up agenda cards, and an L-shaped path to it along
   the streets (at most one turn).
2. **Stops**: every intersection the mayor steps onto, target included (not his start). At each:
   1. **Incentive**: if a corner is empty, the walker puts 1 incentive on one (they stack).
   2. **Tax**: each company pays 1 coin per project it has on these corners. Can't pay: abandon that
      many of those projects (your pick); the tile leaves the game, workers come back.
3. **Credit**: every company collects its credit rating in coins.
4. **Upgrades**: everyone **except the walker** may spend incentives (mat):
   - Credit rating +1 step for 2 / 3 / 4 incentives (max 3).
   - +1 worker for 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 incentives (max 7).
5. **Agenda**: discard the target and reveal a new card, if any are left.

## End of the game

The game ends after the walk to the **last agenda card**. Then:

1. **Final sale**: every company may finish one project at full value.
2. **Firesale**: every other project with a worker sells for **1 coin per level** (Park 0). No
   count.
3. **Most coins wins.**
